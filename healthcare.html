Neurophysiology, cardiology, consultations, physiotherapy & digital health.

A focused healthcare portfolio combining diagnostic neurophysiology, cardiology monitoring, clinical consultation services, physiotherapy, LMIS and AI-assisted healthcare workflows.

Neurophysiology

Diagnostic procedures

Core neurophysiology procedures presented as a clear service catalogue.

EEG

Routine EEG for clinical neurophysiology assessment.

Sleep / Prolonged EEG

Extended and sleep-based EEG recordings where clinically indicated.

Video EEG

EEG combined with synchronized video observation for clinical assessment.

NCS / NCV

Nerve conduction studies for upper and lower limb peripheral nerve assessment.

EMG

Electromyography supporting neuromuscular evaluation.

VEP

Visual evoked potential testing for pathway assessment.

BAER / BERA

Auditory evoked response testing.

RNST

Repetitive nerve stimulation for neuromuscular junction assessment.

Cardiology

Cardiac monitoring

Blood pressure monitoring presented separately from neurophysiology procedures.

ABPM

Ambulatory blood pressure monitoring for extended blood pressure recording and clinical assessment.
